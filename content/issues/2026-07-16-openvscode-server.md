Due to the upstream project no longer updating their app, we have decided to deprecate our [Openvscode-server](https://github.com/linuxserver/docker-openvscode-server) image. We recommend switching to the [Code-server](https://github.com/linuxserver/docker-code-server) or the [VSCodium-web](https://github.com/linuxserver/docker-vscodium-web) image.
