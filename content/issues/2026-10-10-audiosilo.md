We have released a new container for [audiosilo](https://github.com/linuxserver/docker-audiosilo)!

[AudioSilo](https://audiosilo.app) is a self-hosted audiobook server. Point it at your audiobook folders and listen from the built-in web player or the AudioSilo app for iOS, with listening progress synced across devices.
