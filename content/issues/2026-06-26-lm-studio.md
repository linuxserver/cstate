We have released a new container for [LM Studio](https://github.com/linuxserver/docker-lm-studio/)!

[LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai/) can run local AI models like gpt-oss, Llama, Gemma, Qwen, and DeepSeek privately on your computer. This image is a KDE desktop with LM Studio, llmster, and many CLI coding/automation agents pre-installed.
